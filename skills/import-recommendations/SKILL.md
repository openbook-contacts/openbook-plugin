# Import recommendations

Use this when the person wants the plumbers, tutors, sitters and doctors their community recommends kept in one group, like Maple Court • Recommended.

**Three turns, not thirty.** Do steps 1 to 9 before saying anything, ask every question in the one message of step 10, then on their answer do steps 11 and 12 without stopping. Built with an email group's own subscribers (`import-from-email`), the two share one pass and one message. If the app ends a turn before the job is done, end with what's done, where to pick up and “Say continue and I'll pick up there”, and start there next turn, never from the beginning.

1. **Where.** Call `my_access`. Suggest a group named for it, like Recommended, in the community's directory or in Personal, and make it if needed (`manage_groups` `create` with the `directory`, or `create_organization`). A Recommended group isn't the one made from the email group, so leave `email_group` off it: set that only on a group that really is the email group's own (`import-from-email`).
2. **Find the messages** with this app's own access to the person's email, or what they paste or forward: the email group's posts (in Gmail, like list:maplecourt@groups.io), and the messages the person sent and received about them, replies sent to them directly too.
3. **Find its hashtags first.** Many email groups put hashtags in a post's subject, like “[maplecourt] #recommendation Plumber for an old house?”. Read every message's subject alone, in the largest pages the mail search gives, never its body, and count each word starting with #, however it's capitalized. Collect these unless the person said otherwise, and name the counts in step 10: “#recommendation 212, #ISO 96”.
   - Recommendations: #recommendation, #recommendations, #rec, #recs, #reco, #recommend and #referral.
   - Requests, whose replies carry the recommendations: #ISO (“in search of”), #lookingfor, #ask, #question, #help and #wanted. Read the whole thread.
   - An email group without hashtags: phrases like “can anyone recommend”, “does anyone know a good”, “highly recommend” and “we used”, in subjects and posts.
4. **For each recommendation, take:** who's recommended (a business or a person), what for (plumber, math tutor), their phone, email, website (`url`) and address as the post gives them, who recommended them, when, and a short line of why in the recommender's own words. Read only what the recommender wrote, never text they quoted (lines starting with “>”, and everything after “On … wrote:”).
5. **Keep it to recommendations.** Leave out requests nobody answered, and #ask, #question or #help posts that don't name anyone to call. Tell the person about any warnings or complaints you find, and let them decide: notes are read by everyone the group's Notes rule shows them to.
6. **One contact per business or person.** Merge repeat recommendations into one contact, with a note for each.
7. **Numbers on the contact, never in the note:** every phone, email, website and address the post gives goes on the contact as a field, a business's number labeled `work` and `mobile` only for a person's own cell, since mobile numbers identify people. A recommendation with no number still goes in, and its note never carries one (“Phone cited in thread: …” belongs in the phone field).
8. **Look up what the post leaves out.** A recommendation that names a business or someone who works for themselves, like “Dan Ortiz, the handyman”, with no phone, email or website: search the web for it, like “Dan Ortiz handyman” with the community's town, and fill in its fields from its own website, its business listing on a map or review site, or its license record, only where the name, the work and the area all match. Take a business's phone, email, website and address, and a person's only as they publish them for their work, never from a people-search site or a personal profile. Keep where each came from to show in the confirm, never in a note. When nothing clearly matches, add it with what the post gave, and say so.
9. **Who sees what:** these numbers are meant to be called, so suggest Visible for phone, email and website, with Notes Visible. Set the rules before adding.
10. **Ask once.** One message with everything: the count and a few examples, where the group goes and its rules, what you found on the web and where, any warnings or complaints, and any recommended business that may be a contact already, each with your suggestion: “I found 37 recommendations under #recommendation and in replies to #ISO since January, like Rivera Plumbing, (415) 555-0161, which Maya Lee said fixed her water heater the same day. 6 had no number in the posts, and I found 5 of them on their own websites, like Ortiz Handyman, (415) 555-0148. Frank's Water Service is in Personal already, so I'll add that one to the group as it is. I'll put them in Maple Court • Recommended with phone, email and website visible, each with its note. Say yes, or tell me what to change.”
11. **Add each with its note, in one call.** `import_contacts` (up to 500 a call, as an owner) or `add_contact`, each contact's `note` the recommendation, which everyone the group's Notes rule shows notes to reads, so no `add_note` after: one note per recommendation, joined with a line between for a business recommended more than once, like “Recommended by Maya Lee, March 2026: fixed our water heater the same day.”, or with no reason given, “Recommended by Maya Lee, March 2026, for furnace repair.” A business already in the directory gets the note once its import joins it. Merge what the person said is one business with `merge_contacts` in one call, from the `possible_duplicates` the import reports. That's the whole note:
    - **No hedging.** A recommendation is one person's word, and naming them says so: never add “not independently verified”, “unconfirmed” or “verify before hiring”.
    - **Nothing about where you found it:** not which email group, thread, email or search, and no reminder to check something. A name or number you're unsure of is a question in step 10, never a caveat on the card.
    - **No number, email or address:** those are fields (step 7).
    - **Only on the contact recommended,** never on the recommender's own contact: what someone posted isn't a note about them.
12. **Report** how many contacts and notes went in, and that people in the group can now search them, for example “who fixes water heaters?” (`search-contacts`).
