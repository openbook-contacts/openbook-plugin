# Share details before anyone asks

Sharing before anyone asks happens on Openbook's pages, not through a tool here.

1. **Their own details:** give them `share_details_page` from `my_access`, their Share My Details page. There they pick the fields, like their mobile number or an email, and share them with everyone who sees their contact through a group they're in, or with one person, even where the group makes people request them or hides them.
2. **A contact's details:** on an iPhone, iPad or Mac with Openbook, the contact's card has Share Details, which opens the same page for that contact. Say so, rather than offering to share it here.
3. **See or stop what they've shared:** the same page lists it, with Stop sharing.
4. **Never say whether a number uses Openbook.** The page doesn't either.
5. **If they want someone to ask them instead,** that's a request: see the answer-requests skill.
