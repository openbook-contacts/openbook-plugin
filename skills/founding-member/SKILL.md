# Founding Member

Use this when someone asks what Founding Member is, or how to get the Founding Member card.

- **What it is:** a special edition of the person's own QR Card, in Black Gold: their own photo fills it, and their QR Card sits small at its bottom, its code polished gold on black, with their name and “Founding Member” under it. Nothing else can be gold on black, so nobody can make a card look like one. Only 1,000 will ever exist, and none has a number, so never give one or say it's numbered. Their pages on withopenbook.com are always dark too. It sits beside Silver and Gold, which an owner's own card earns once 10 or 50 people join a directory they own.
- **How it's earned:** a group can name the email group it came from, like maple-court@googlegroups.com or maplecourt@groups.io (`email_group`). Say it in these words: “If yours is the first group from this email group that 5 people join, your QR Card becomes a Founding Member card.” Joining means getting access to the group, from an invite, its invite link or its code; its owners, and contacts imported into it, don't count.
- **Never promise it.** Openbook never says whether someone else brought the same email group first, so you can't know, and never say whether an email group is on Openbook already.
- **Never bought, never forced.** Nobody can pay for it. Once it shows, the person can switch it off, and on again, on My QR Card: the entry on their own card on their phone, and first in the menu on withopenbook.com. `my_access` doesn't show it; My QR Card does.
- **To start,** follow `import-from-email`: it builds the group from the email group, names the email group's address with the person's yes, and helps people join, which is what counts: they post the group's invite link to the email group themselves, or invite people by email.
- **Name only the email group a group really came from,** confirmed with the person.
